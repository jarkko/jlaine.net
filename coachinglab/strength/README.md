# Strength & physical preparation

Strength and conditioning reference material for the lab. Distinct in domain from the CLA / nonlinear-pedagogy content (which is skill/tactics), but the same content-first, sourced approach: every training claim is backed by a citation in the text.

Finnish-first, English mirror follows — same convention as the rest of the lab.

## Contents

- `voimaharjoittelu-nuorille-suunnistajille.md` — strength training demo for young orienteers (U14/U16/U18), Finnish (primary). ~30 min session per group, myth-busting, weekly programming, DOMS, calf-loading evidence note, and the NTNU/Helgerud max-strength case study (Huovila).
- `strength-training-young-orienteers.md` — English mirror of the above.
- `*.pdf` — rendered handouts for each language (pandoc + wkhtmltopdf). Regenerate from the matching `.md` after edits.

## Provenance

Drafted iteratively in `~/Dropbox/Lentopallo/aluevalmennus/`, then moved here — this is now the canonical home for the material. PDFs were built with pandoc using the `wkhtmltopdf` engine (`RBENV_VERSION=3.1.2`) and inline CSS styling.

## Key sources referenced in the material

- Lloyd et al. 2014 — youth resistance training international consensus
- Lloyd & Oliver 2012 — Youth Physical Development model
- Støren et al. 2008 (PMID 18460997); Hoff, Gran & Helgerud 2002 (PMID 12383074); Heggelund et al. 2013 (PMID 23307029) — NTNU maximal-strength-for-endurance line
- Helgerud et al. 2007 (PMID 17414804) — the 4×4 aerobic interval method (context / segue)
- Revak et al. 2017; Komi 1990/1992; Bohm et al. 2015 — Achilles/calf loading
- Afonso et al. 2021; Weppler & Magnusson 2010; Herbert, de Noronha & Kamper 2011 — stretching, range of motion, and DOMS
