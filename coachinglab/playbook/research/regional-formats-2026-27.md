# Regional junior formats (U13, U11, U9), 2026–27

Research done 2026-11-01 for the playbook's small-court section. Every claim
below is marked VERIFIED (quoted from a primary source) or INFERRED.

**Summary:**
- **Regions:** Lentopalloliitto has five: Etelä, Lounais, Länsi, Itä and Pohjois (VERIFIED, lentopallo.fi/alueet).
  - Länsi covers Etelä-Pohjanmaa, Keski-Pohjanmaa and Keski-Suomi.
  - Pohjois covers Lappi, Pohjois-Pohjanmaa and Kainuu.
- **U13 and U11:** all five regions play 3v3, at an AM/aluesarja level and a Tiikeri level. Tiikeri is played as Triplapallo.
- **U13 6v6:** four regions run a 6v6 series that U13 players can enter. Only Pohjois publishes full rules for it.
- **4v4:** no region plays 4v4 in U13, U11 or U9.
- **U9:** run by the federation, not the regions.

## Triplapallo mode by region

The three modes in Minisäännöt §5:
1. Tripla
2. 3 kosketuksen tripla
3. Hiha-Tripla

| Region | Mode |
|---|---|
| Etelä | 1, with no catches. A coach's throw that goes straight back over the net on the first touch is a fault. VERIFIED, ES-T: "pelataan minisääntöjen pelimuodolla TRIPLA… ensimmäisen kosketuksen mennessä suoraan verkon yli tuomitaan se virheeksi… Koppeja EI… sallita" |
| Lounais | 1, with the same first-touch rule. VERIFIED, LO-D/E: "pelimuotoa nro 1 … ensimmäinen kosketus ei saa palautua suoraan verkon yli" |
| Pohjois | U11 Tiikeri: mode 1. U11 aluesarja: mode 2 in autumn, normal play in spring. U13 Tiikeri: mode 1 in autumn 2026, mode 2 in spring 2027. VERIFIED, PO-R; PO-U: "Sarjassa pelataan 1. Tripla-säännöillä" |
| Itä, Länsi | "Triplapallo", mode not named. Not found |

No region publishes Hiha-Tripla (mode 3) as its mode.

## 4v4

- None in U13, U11 or U9.
- Etelä runs a U18 4v4 "Tsemppisarja" for beginners, "noudattaen liiton sarjasääntöjen aikaisempaa D4vs4-pelitapaa". VERIFIED, ES-T.
- The national Minisäännöt still lists "U13 (3vs3/4vs4) 200 cm" in the net-height table. INFERRED: leftover text.

## Other regional deviations from the national Minisäännöt

- **Shorter U11 court:**
  - Etelä, VERIFIED, ES-T: "U11:ssä takarajana on sulkapallokentän lyhempi raja".
  - Pohjois, VERIFIED, PO-R: "Kentän koko 6,1m x 5,94m molemmissa sarjoissa". INFERRED: per court half, so the full court is about 11.88 × 6.1 m.
  - The national diagram (Kuva 1) shows 13.4 × 6.1 m, with a shorter serve line only for U11 Tiikeri.
- **Serve and reception:** free (overhand serve and finger reception allowed) in every region, as in the national rules. VERIFIED in every source.
- **Itä:** in U13/U11 girls' series, one boy per team may play per set if the club has no boys' team. VERIFIED, IT-S.
- **Länsi:** "U13-U11-ikäisissä, liberon käyttö ei ole sallittua". Net heights match the national ones: U13 200, U11 180, U9 160 ±10 cm. VERIFIED, LA-S.
- **Pohjois:** the U13 6v6 net is 220 cm (see below).

## U13 6v6 rules

**National baseline (Nuorten sarjamääräykset, 22.9.2026), VERIFIED:**
- "U13-ikäiset 6vs6 200 cm" (girls and boys).
- No libero: "U13, U11 ja U9… liberon käyttö ei ole sallittua".
- Minimum hall height 6 m.
- Minisäännöt: the tie-break serve rule applies "(myös 6 vs 6-peleissä)".

**Pohjois U13 6v6 (spring 2027; three tournaments, held if at least 4 teams enter), VERIFIED, PO-R:**
- **Format:** a 6v6 version of 3 kosketuksen tripla, played in cycles of three rallies. The coach throws to the player in zone 6, about 2 m behind the attack line. "Mikäli pallo joudutaan toimittamaan 1. tai 2. kosketuksella vastustajan puolelle… virhe".
- **Rotation:** after each cycle the serve changes and teams rotate as normal ("normaalin 6v6 rotaation").
- **Stoppages:** unlimited substitutions; two timeouts per set, taken when the serve changes.
- **Equipment and court:** serve from the 9 m line (teams may agree to move it closer); net **220 cm**; court 9 × 18; Mikasa V400W ball.
- **Sets:** best of three, to 25, third set to 15.
- **Roles:** specialised roles are not intended; teams that want them are pointed to the U13–U17 harrastesarja.

**Etelä U14 6v6 (teaching series, U13 players allowed), VERIFIED, ES-T:**
- "noudatetaan U13-pelisääntöjä verkkokorkeuden, tiebreak-syöttösäännön ja keskiviivan ylityssäännön osalta".
- A shorter serve line can be agreed per player, except in the spring final (places 1–6).
- Two over-age players (born 2012) allowed.
- Coaches get extra time and timeouts to teach.

**Itä U13–U17 6v6 harrastesarja, VERIFIED, IT-6:**
- "Säännöt ovat U15-ikäisten säännöt seuraavin poikkeuksin": the U13 serve line and serve rule may be used, the net may be lowered, and there are four timeouts per set.
- Mixed teams and two-club teams are allowed; no standings are kept.
- INFERRED: under U15 rules, the libero and overlap rules apply. The page doesn't say so.

**Lounais Lounalentis:**
- VERIFIED, LO-D: big court, mini ball, free serve and reception.
- Net height, overlap, substitutions and libero are not stated. INFERRED: national U13 rules, so a 200 cm net and no libero.

**Länsi U13 6v6 mixed series:**
- Five weekends, no libero, free serve and reception.
- The Länsi Minituomarikoulutus (2025) says: "Kaikissa U13 (D) sarjoissa (myös 6 vs 6) on käytössä vapaa aloitus… vapaa vastaanotto" (VERIFIED). No other rules found.

**Pohjois U13–U15–U17 6v6 harrastesarja:** only rules on transferring players are published. Pohjois also runs a "Nuorten sekaharrastesarja" for players born 2010–14; its format isn't stated.
