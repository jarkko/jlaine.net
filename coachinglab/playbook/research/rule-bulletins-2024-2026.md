## (a) Does the new timing apply to junior series?

**U20/U17/U15: yes, from 1.11.2026.** INFERRED from verified text. No source names the junior series explicitly.
- §16.5 applies "Lentopalloliiton alaisissa kaikissa muissa sarjoissa".
- Sarjamääräykset §1: junior series define in their own regulations only "yleisistä sarjamääräyksistä poikkeavat asiat".
- Nuorten §1: "U20-, U17 ja U15-ikäluokissa noudatetaan pääsääntöisesti Kansainvälisen Lentopalloliiton virallisia pelisääntöjä".
- The junior regulations (22.9 and 26.10) have no exception on positioning.
- The 31.7.2026 interpretation already said "kaikissa Lentopalloliiton alaisissa sarjoissa".
- Nuorten §4.2 "vapaa aloitus ja vastaanotto" is not about positioning: it means an overhand serve and "hiha- ja sormivastaanotto sallittu".

**U13 6v6: probably yes.** INFERRED, lower confidence.
- U13 follows the minilentopallo rules, which give positioning only for 3v3.
- The general sarjamääräykset name U9–U13 elsewhere (§16.1, §16.5.6), so they apply to those series too.
- No source states this for U13 6v6.

## (b) What may the receiving team do after the server starts?

**VERIFIED:**
- Players must be in correct positions from the whistle until the server starts the service action (a step, the tossing hand starting to move, or a foot movement).
- From the 3.4.2026 bulletin: "se saa liikkua heti, kun syöttösuoritus aloitetaan… saavat poistua oikeilta pelipaikoiltaan".
- The only remaining limit: everyone except the server must be inside their own court at the service hit.
- The 13.9.2024 screening rules still apply.

**INFERRED:**
- Receivers may switch places, and so move into a new receive formation, as soon as the server's first movement begins.
- Leaving the court before the hit is a fault.
- No federation coaching material on what this means for receive formations was found.

## (c) Contradictions and what superseded what

1. The 13.8.2025 interpretation:
   - moved the check to the toss;
   - removed the in-court requirement at the hit.
2. The 31.7.2026 note restored the in-court requirement for 2026–27. The check stayed at the toss.
3. The 1.9.2026 bulletin ("toistaiseksi viime kauden… tulkinnoilla") is ambiguous against the 31.7.2026 note.
4. 30.9.2026 / §16.5.3 supersedes both. The check now ends when the service action starts, not at the toss:
   - in force in ML from 2.10;
   - everywhere else from 1.11.2026.
5. So in non-ML series between 30.9 and 31.10.2026, the check was still at the toss.
6. The 31.7.2026 text on the interpretations page has not been updated.
