# Volleyball rules in force for 2026–27: research report for updating the LP Kangasala *Junioreiden pelikirja* (2018)

Research date: 2026-11-01. All PDFs and pages are saved under `(local download, not kept in the repo) `, as PDFs with pdftotext `.txt` copies or as `.html` pages.

Confidence key: **high** = I read the primary text myself. **medium** = primary source, but the wording is ambiguous or sources conflict. **low** = indirect.

## 0. Summary: what has changed since the 2018 playbook

| 2018 playbook assumption | Status for 2026–27 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| All six players must be in rotational order before the serve | **Out of date.** Since 1.1.2025 (FIVB 2025–2028, Rule 7.4), only the **receiving** team has to be in rotational order. Serving-team players "are free to occupy any position at the service hit", but must still be inside their own court (the server excepted). | FIVB Rules 2025–2028 7.4 |
| Moment of the overlap check = the service hit | **Changed in Finland this autumn.** Since the 30.9.2026 bulletin, the receiving team must be correctly positioned **from the 1st referee's whistle until the server starts the service action** (a step, the arm starting the toss, or a foot movement for the serve). It is no longer checked at the hit. This applies in Mestaruusliiga from 2.10.2026 and in all other Lentopalloliitto series from **1.11.2026**. All players of both teams except the server must still be inside their own court at the service hit. | Lentopalloliitto tiedote 30.9.2026; Sarjamääräykset 2026–27 §16.5.3 |
| Front row 4-3-2, back row 5-6-1; pairs 4-5, 3-6, 2-1 | **Still correct for the receiving team.** The test is feet in contact with the floor, and the last floor contact fixes the position. Front/back is checked only between the corresponding pairs. Left/right is checked only within each row. There is no diagonal check. | FIVB 7.4.1–7.4.3 |
| F-juniors: catch-and-throw on a small court | **Still broadly true, under a new name.** F is now **U9 (MiniLentis)**, always **2v2**. Recommended court 9 × 4.5 m, net 160 cm ±10. **Tiikerisarja** allows 2 catches (1st and 2nd contact). **Supersarja** allows 1 catch, and the first contact must be played directly. | Minisäännöt 2026–27 §6 |
| E-juniors: 3 per side | **Still 3v3, under a new name.** E is now **U11**. New for 2026–27: the court is the **badminton doubles court (13.4 × 6.1 m)**. Net 180 cm, size-4 ball (Mikasa V400W). Tiikeri level plays Triplapallo. | Minisäännöt 2026–27 §2, §3.2, §5 |
| D-juniors: 4 per side | **Out of date (at least in the Länsi region).** D is now **U13**. Regional championship and Tiikeri series are **3v3** on a badminton doubles court, net 200 cm. In Länsi there is also a separate **U13 6v6 mixed series** in 2026–27. 4v4 only appears in a net-height line ("3vs3/4vs4 200 cm"). | Minisäännöt 2026–27; Lännen tiedote 8/2026; Nuorten sarjamääräykset 2026–27 |
| (implicit) unlimited / 6 substitutions | **8 substitutions per set** in Finland from 2.10.2026 (Mestaruusliiga) and 1.11.2026 (other series). FIVB 15.1 still says six. | Tiedote 30.9.2026; Sarjamääräykset §16.5.4 |
| (implicit) tips and open-hand attacks | **Tighter from 2026–27.** A tip must be quick and made with one hand and/or the fingers, or as an overhand finger pass. Carries, pushes, and long or prolonged two-hand overhand-finger attacks are forbidden. Long carries in blocking situations are forbidden too. | Sarjamääräykset §16.5.1 |

Other 2025–26 changes coaches should know: serving-team players may not raise their hands above their heads during the serve (12.5.3). After the 2nd or 3rd team hit, a ball sent through the external space is "out" (10.1.2.3). Overhand finger double contact is allowed on the 2nd team contact if it is a single action and not an attack hit (Finnish interpretation since 2025, still in force). Libero front-zone finger set: the last foot contact decides (casebook 5.27). For 2026–27 there is also a separate 90 s serve warm-up, and the ball may touch the ceiling after the 1st or 2nd contact if it stays on the same side.

**Watch out:** the lentopallo.fi bulletin dated **1.4.2026** ("Tärkeä tiedote kaikille sääntömuutoksista…") is an April Fools' joke (shout votes, "dubbelpoints", libero attacks from anywhere). Do not cite it.

## A. FIVB indoor 6v6 rules in force for 2026–27

**Edition in force:** *Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028*, approved by the 39th FIVB World Congress 2024, "To be implemented in all competitions beginning after 1st January 2025". The FIVB rules page currently links English **v05**: https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf (high).

**No 2026 amendment to the rulebook text was found.** In 2026 FIVB issued *rule tests*, not amendments. The FIVB Board approved them on 27.2.2026 for VNL 2026, the U17 World Championships and the 2026 Continental Championships, and "any update to the Official Volleyball Rules will be reviewed" after testing. Source: https://www.fivb.com/fivb-board-of-administration-approves-rule-tests-for-2026-competitions/ (high that these are tests). CEV adopted all the tests for its 2026–27 season on 23.9.2026, and Finland followed (see B). So the autumn-2026 changes the user mentioned are **CEV/Finnish adoptions of the FIVB 2026 tests**. They are not a new FIVB rulebook (high).

### A1. Rule 7.4 positions at the service hit

- **7.4 (current text):** "At the moment the ball is hit by the server, each team must be positioned within its own court (except the server). The players of the receiving team must be in the rotational order at the service hit. The players of the serving team, however, are free to occupy any position at the service hit."
- **7.4.4:** "After the service hit, the players of both teams may move around and occupy any position on their court and the free zone."
- **Change from 2021–24:** the old text said each team must be "within its own court in the rotational order (except the server)". Source: FIVB modification table, version 20250206 (vbahk.org.hk lead, verified against the EN v05 rulebook).
- **Guidelines 2025, Rule 7 note:** "The 2024 Congress approved the cancellation of positional faults by the serving team, in order to support their defence of the first attack by the opponent. This cancellation does not apply to the receiving team." Source: FIVB/CEV *Refereeing Guidelines and Instructions 2025*, https://inside.cev.eu/media/bm4jik3r/new-guidelines-instructions-2025.pdf
- **2025 test:** the moment of the receiving team's fault was moved to the server's toss at all FIVB events in 2025. Same source: "the moment the toss of the ball by the server for service will define the fault".
- **2026 test, "Out of rotation error – free positioning of the receiving team":** receivers must be in rotation at the whistle but may move once the serving action starts (FIVB news 27.2.2026). The rulebook text is unchanged.
- Effective: rulebook 1.1.2025; tests as listed above. **Confidence: high.**

### A2. How position is judged

- **7.4.1:** front row = 4 (front-left), 3 (front-centre), 2 (front-right); back row = 5 (back-left), 6 (back-centre), 1 (back-right).
- **7.4.2.1:** "each back-row player must be positioned further back from the centre line than the corresponding front-row player". Corresponding pairs are 4–5, 3–6 and 2–1.
- **7.4.2.2:** front-row players among themselves, and back-row players among themselves, must be "positioned laterally in the order indicated in Rule 7.4.1". That is 4-3-2 and 5-6-1.
- **7.4.3:** positions are "determined and controlled according to the positions of their feet contacting the ground (the last contact with the floor fixes the player's position)".
- **7.4.3.1:** each back-row player must be "level with or have at least a part of one foot further from the centre line than the front foot of the corresponding front-row player".
- **7.4.3.2:** each right (left) player must be "level with or have at least a part of one foot closer to the right (left) side line than the foot further from the right (left) side line of the other players in that row".
- **Diagonals** (e.g. 4–1, 2–5): the rule only compares corresponding front/back pairs and players within the same row, so there is no diagonal constraint. This follows from the text of 7.4.2. I found no casebook item stating it in so many words.
- From 2025 the serving team is exempt from all of these checks (A1).
- URL: EN v05 above; Finnish text: https://www.lentopallotuomarit.fi/saannot/07_PelinRakenne.htm. **Confidence: high.**

### A3. Positional fault (7.5), rotational fault (7.7), precedence

Note the numbering: 7.6 is ROTATION and 7.7 is ROTATIONAL FAULT.

- **7.5.1:** "The team commits a positional fault, if any player is not in his/her correct position at the moment the ball is hit by the server." A player on court through an illegal substitution counts as a positional fault with the consequences of an illegal substitution.
- **7.5.4:** a point and service to the opponent, and positions must be rectified.
- **7.6.2:** after winning the right to serve, the team rotates one position clockwise (2→1, 1→6, etc.).
- **7.7.1:** "A rotational fault is committed when the SERVICE is not made according to the rotational order." The opponent gets a point and the next service. If the fault is found only after the rally, only a single point is awarded. Under **7.7.2**, points scored by the faulty team from the moment of the fault are cancelled if that moment can be determined.
- **Precedence:**
  - **7.5.2 / 12.7.1:** a server's fault at the moment of the service hit (improper execution, wrong rotational order) is counted before a positional fault.
  - **7.5.3 / 12.7.2:** if the service is correct but becomes faulty afterwards (out, net, screen), the positional fault is counted first.
  - **12.6.1** "even if the opponent is out of position"; **12.6.2** "(unless a player is out of position)".
- **2026–27 note:** in Finland the receiving team's positional check is now made from the whistle until the start of the service action (B4). The rulebook does not say how precedence works under the new timing. **UNKNOWN** whether a referee instruction addresses it.
- **Confidence: high** (rule text).

### A4. Libero

- **19.1.1:** up to two Liberos. In FIVB/World/Official senior competitions, a team with more than 12 players must have two. **19.1.3:** "Only one Libero may be on court at any time."
- **19.3.1.1:** the Libero may replace any back-row player.
- **19.3.1.2:** the Libero may not complete an attack hit from anywhere if the ball is entirely above the net at contact.
- **19.3.1.3:** the Libero may not serve, block or attempt to block.
- **19.3.1.4:** "A player may not complete an attack hit when the ball is entirely higher than the top of the net, if the ball is coming from an overhand finger pass by a Libero in his/her front zone. The ball may be freely attacked if the Libero makes the same action from outside his/her front zone." Also 13.3.6.
- **Finnish interpretation of 5.8.2025, based on casebook 5.27:** if the Libero's last foot contact was in the front zone, the finger pass counts as from the front zone even if that foot is lifted. Attacking it above the net is then a fault under 13.3.6 / 19.3.1.4. Source: https://www.lentopallotuomarit.fi/home/tuomaritoiminta/lentopalloliiton-tuomarijaoksen-saantotulkintoja/ (high).
- **19.3.2.1:** Libero replacements are not substitutions and are unlimited, but there must be a completed rally between two replacements. **19.3.2.2:** the Acting Libero can only be replaced by the regular replacement player or by the second Libero. **19.3.2.7:** replacements go through the Libero Replacement Zone.
- **2025–28 changes:**
  - **19.4.2.1 / 19.4.2.3:** the assistant coach may also act for the coach when re-designating a Libero (modification table).
  - **19.2:** the re-designated Libero should wear the same style and colour of jersey but keep their own number (FIVB competitions).
- **2026 test (rosters):** 12–14 players, at least one Libero, and a second may be nominated. This replaces "two mandatory if more than 12". Finland's version is in B1.
- **Confidence: high.**

### A5. Back-row attack, back-row block, attack line, tips

- **1.3.4 attack line:** its rear edge is drawn 3 m back from the centre-line axis. The front zone runs from the centre-line axis to the rear edge of the attack line.
- **13.2.2:** "A back-row player may complete an attack hit at any height from behind the front zone". At take-off the feet must neither touch nor cross the attack line, and the player may land in the front zone.
- **13.2.3:** a back-row player may also attack from the front zone "if at the moment of the contact a part of the ball is lower than the top of the net".
- **13.2.4 / 13.3.4:** no player may attack the opponent's service while the ball is in the front zone and entirely above the net.
- **13.3.3:** it is a fault if a back-row player completes an attack hit from the front zone with the ball entirely above the net.
- **13.1.3:** an attack hit is completed when the ball completely crosses the vertical plane of the net or is touched by an opponent.
- **14.1.1:** "Only front-row players are permitted to complete a block". **14.6.2:** a fault if "A back-row player or a Libero completes a block or participates in a completed block". **14.6.6:** a fault if the Libero attempts a block. **14.5:** blocking the service is forbidden.
- **Tips:**
  - **13.1.2:** "During an attack hit, tipping is permitted only if the ball is cleanly hit, and not caught or thrown." **9.2.2:** "The ball must not be caught and/or thrown."
  - **Guidelines 2025:** a tip is executed "gently, with the fingers of one hand, where the direction of the ball is changed only once during the action and the time in contact with the ball is short in time and in distance".
  - **2026 test "Push–carry–catch–throw-tip":** strict application of 9.2.2. Only a very short tip. No direction changes, two-hand attacks, pushes, open-hand block-outs or carries (FIVB news 27.2.2026).
  - **Finland 2026–27 (Sarjamääräykset §16.5.1):** "Jujun ('tipin') pitää olla yhdellä kädellä ja/tai sormilla tai sormilyönnillä tehty suoritus." Two clarifications: the setter's 2nd-contact dump/attack is still allowed "kunhan palloa ei saateta, kopata tai heitetä". A 2nd- or 3rd-contact overhand-finger attack must be "puhdas" according to the series' interpretation level.
  - So open-hand tips are legal only as quick, clean contacts. Pushes, carries and "open-hand block-outs" are now faults in Finland.
- **Confidence: high** (rule text). **Medium** on exactly where Finnish referees draw the line in junior series.

### A6. Other 2025–28 and autumn-2026 changes (brief)

From the FIVB 2025–28 rulebook (effective 1.1.2025; high):

1. **7.4 / 7.4.4:** the serving team is free from positional faults.
2. **10.1.2:** only a ball coming from the team's **1st** hit that crossed into the opponent's free zone through the external space may be played back. **10.1.2.3:** if it came from the 2nd or 3rd hit, it is "out" the moment it crosses the net plane. If it is played back but does not return through the external space on the same side, it is "out".
3. **11.1.2:** "**After** an attack hit" (was "During") a player may pass the hand beyond the net.
4. **12.5.3 (new):** "Any player of the serving team is forbidden to raise hands above the head during service, until the ball has passed beyond the net." The 1st referee may caution the team via the game captain.
5. **9.1.2.3:** a simultaneous extended contact over the net continues even if completed above the opponent's court.
6. **23.3.2.3 / 24.3.2.8 / 29.2.1.3:** referees and line judges now also judge the **2nd** hit passing over or outside the antenna.
7. **19.4.2:** the assistant coach role in Libero re-designation.
8. **Unchanged but relevant:**
   - **12.4.4:** 8 seconds to serve after the whistle.
   - **1.4.2:** service zone 9 m wide behind the end line.
   - **12.4.3:** at the hit or jump take-off the server must not touch the court or the floor outside the service zone.
   - **15.1:** two time-outs and **six** substitutions per set.
9. **Interpretation since 25.7.2025 (CEV), in Finland "välittömästi" (August 2025):** on the team's **2nd** contact, consecutive contacts in an overhand finger pass are allowed if there is only one playing action and it does not become an attack hit. Source: Lentopalloliiton tuomarijaos 5.8.2025, and Nuorten sarjamääräykset 2026–27 §19.1 (high).

FIVB 2026 tests (27.2.2026). All were adopted by CEV, and by Finland as applicable (B):

- Receiving-team positioning (A1)
- 8 substitutions
- Roster 12–14 with at least one Libero
- Ceiling contact after the 1st or 2nd contact is legal if the ball stays on the same side
- Strict tip/push rule
- Mid-rally challenge bookmark
- Challenges for defence and serve-receive touches
- No time-out after a lost challenge
- No whistle for clear faults
- The coach may speak to the 1st referee (VNL layout)
- Separate 90 s serve warm-up per team
- Second-contact double-contact interpretation unchanged

### B2. Does Finland apply the serving-team exemption in all series, including juniors?

- **Adult and SM series:** yes. The FIVB rules apply (Sarjamääräykset §1), and the Finnish translation of 7.4 reads: "Syöttävän joukkueen pelaajat voivat sijoittua vapaasti mille tahansa pelipaikalle syöttöhetkellä." Source: lentopallotuomarit.fi/saannot/07_PelinRakenne.htm; also the Länsi-Suomi referee training slides *Säännöt v3.3* (31.8.2025). §16.5.3 adds that serving-team players other than the server must be inside their own court at the serve. **High.**
- **U20/U17/U15 (6v6):** FIVB rules apply "pääsääntöisesti", and no junior exception on positions was found, so yes. **Medium-high.** No document states it for juniors explicitly.
- **Does §16.5.3 (new timing) apply in junior series from 1.11.2026?** The wording "kaikissa muissa sarjoissa" and the U9–U13 carve-out in 16.5.6 imply yes. The Nuorten sarjamääräykset (22.9.2026) predate the decision and do not mention it. **Medium.**
- **U13 6v6 (Länsi mixed series):** U13 follows the minilentopallo rules (Nuorten §1), and the minisäännöt only define positions for 3v3. **UNKNOWN** which overlap rule (FIVB 7.4 with the serving-team exemption, or something else) applies in U13 6v6.

**Triplapallo (U13/U11 Tiikeri, Minisäännöt §5, partly new for 2026–27):**

- Cycle of 3 balls: the serve, then a "jatkopallo" thrown to the receiving team, then a jatkopallo to the serving team.
- The jatkopallo is thrown underhand with two hands, above antenna height, to the player in position 4. It must be played from behind the 3 m line, by finger or forearm pass.
- **New:** if the jatkopallo goes straight over on the first contact, the opponent gets a point.
- **New:** there are three game modes, chosen by the region:
  1. *Tripla*: free contacts.
  2. *3 kosketuksen tripla*: three contacts mandatory; the 1st contact may be forearm or finger.
  3. *Hiha-Tripla*: three contacts mandatory; the 1st contact must be a forearm pass.
- The first ball from the serve in every mode is played "normaalin lentopallon säännöillä".

**Rotation / overlap in small-court games:**

- **U13/U11 3v3 (§3.5) and Triplapallo (§5.6):** "Pelaajien II-, III- ja IV-paikalla tulee aloitussyötön hetkellä sijoittua verkon suuntaisesti oikeaan järjestykseen … Pelaajien etäisyyttä verkosta ei ole määritelty." So only the **left–right order** counts. Triplapallo applies the same rule at the moment of the jatkopallo.
- **Rotation:** the player in II leaves the court, III moves to II and serves, IV moves to III, and the next substitute enters at IV. Everyone on the scoresheet must rotate in. In U11, "Kaikki pelaajat voivat hyökätä verkolta."
- **U9 2v2 (§6.1.6):** "Pelaajat voivat sijoittua kentälle vapaasti joko rinnakkain tai peräkkäin." The player who has been on court longer leaves at each rotation.

**Setter / Libero / specialisation:**

- **Libero:** "U13, U11 ja U9 (D-, E- ja F)-ikäluokissa liberon käyttö ei ole sallittua" (Nuorten §7.9.1). In U20–U15 the Libero is per set and optional.
- **Equal playing time:** "Jokaisen turnauksessa mukana olevan pelaajan tulee saada turnauksessa tasapuolisesti peliaikaa" (Minisäännöt §3.1/3.2). "Pelaajien tasapuolinen peluuttaminen U13–U9" is also on the technical-meeting agenda (Nuorten §7.5).
- **No explicit setter or specialisation ban** was found in any 2026–27 document (UNKNOWN whether one exists elsewhere).

**Conflicts to flag:**

- **U11/U13 underhand hand serve:** Nuorten §4.2 says the hand serve without a toss is allowed "U11-ikäisissä" only. Minisäännöt §3.4 lists it under "U13-nuoret ja U11-nuoret". Minisäännöt §7 says the sarjamääräykset prevail. **Medium.**
- **U9 court:** Minisäännöt §2 and §6 give a recommended 4.5 × 4.5 m, while §6.1.1 and Sarjamääräykset §17 say 9 × 4.5 m. **Medium.**
- **U9 Super catch:** the older text in §6 and §6.1.8 ("1. koppi sallittu, koppi voi olla 2. kosketus") reads differently from the new red-marked §6.1.3 ("ensimmäinen kosketus EI saa olla koppi"). I treat the red-marked text as current. **Medium.**

**Changed for autumn 2026:**

- U11 court enlarged to badminton doubles lines.
- Three Triplapallo modes; U9 game modes (Koppipeli / Nosto-koppipeli) codified.
- Point to the opponent if a jatkopallo goes straight over.
- Coach and bench-staff licences mandatory (U20–U9).
- Länsi: U13 3v3 AM plus a 6v6 mixed series.
- From 1.11.2026 the §16.5 interpretations apply. The serve warm-up may be waived in U9–U13 by agreement.

**PowerCup:** no PowerCup rule document was found on any primary source (UNKNOWN). "Junnulentis" is the federation's junior site (junnulentis.fi), which hosts the minisäännöt. "MiniLentis" is the U9 2v2 game (Minisäännöt §6).

### B4. Finnish referee instructions this season (overlap, Libero, serving)

All of the following are from Lentopalloliiton tuomaritoiminta (tuomaripäällikkö Pasi Hakkarainen) at https://www.lentopallotuomarit.fi/home/tuomaritoiminta/lentopalloliiton-tuomarijaoksen-saantotulkintoja/ and lentopallo.fi.

- **31.7.2026, "CEV tarkennus koskien vastaanottavan joukkueen sijoittumista":**
  - "Kaudella 25-26 oli Lentopalloliiton alaisissa sarjoissa voimassa tulkinta jossa vastaanottavan joukkueen sijoittuminen on vapaa sen jälkeen kun pallo on heitettu…"
  - "Sarjakaudella 26-27 tämä tulkinta on kuitenkin voimassa kaikissa Lentopalloliiton alaisissa sarjoissa. Vaikka pelaajien sijoittuminen on aloitusheiton jälkeen 'vapaa', niin molempien joukkueiden pitää olla oman kenttäpuolensa sisällä aloituksen lyönnin hetkellä."
  - **High.**
- **30.9.2026 bulletin / Sarjamääräykset §16.5.3:** "Vastaanottavan joukkueen tulee olla sijoittuneena oikein päätuomarin aloituslupavihellyksen hetkestä siihen hetkeen, kunnes syöttäjä aloittaa syöttösuorituksen." The start of the service action is "mm. se, että syöttäjä ottaa askeleen, syöttäjän käsi aloittaa liikkeen pallon heittämiseksi tai jalka tekee liikkeen aloitusyöttöä varten". Also: "Pelaajien (myös syöttävän joukkueen pl. aloitusta syöttävä pelaaja) pitää olla oman pelikenttänsä sisäpuolella aloitussyötön hetkellä." Effective 2.10.2026 in ML and 1.11.2026 elsewhere. **High.**
- **History (for context):**
  - 13.8.2025: the check moment moved to the server's toss in all Finnish series.
  - 13.9.2024: "Mitään ylimääräistä 'toleranssia' ei tulisi sallia."
  - 13.9.2024 screening rule: serving-team hands must be below the top of the head until the ball passes the net plane. Hands behind the head for protection are allowed.
  - 13.9.2024 rhythm: service permission within about 15 s of the previous rally ending, then 8 s to serve.
- **Libero:** 5.8.2025 front-zone finger-set interpretation (casebook 5.27, see A4). Libero designation changes in §16.5.5 / §7.6.1 (B1).
- **Serving:** the separate 90 s serve warm-up protocol (A team first; in ML the away team first):
  - −18 min: toss
  - −17 min: net warm-up
  - −8 min: A-team serve warm-up
  - −6:30 min: B-team serve warm-up
  - −5 min: warm-up ends
